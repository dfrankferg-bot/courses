## TLDR

- Blitz **across lanes/languages/formats** (country, sensory/lo-fi, gospel, bilingual Latin, Afrobeats), never across upload count — platforms now punish AI volume spam, and the biggest winner (Gracie's Corner: 10B views, June 2026 Disney deal, 100% ownership retained) uploads ~20 originals a year.
- Launch 2 opposite channels first: a **sensory/lo-fi** channel (cheapest, most automatable, proven Hey Bear comp) and a **country toddler** channel (widest-open identity lane).
- IP is inverted: **trademarks + human-written lyrics + human-finished characters are the moat**; Suno masters are a licensed, replaceable input. Human QC on every video is the line between a brand and terminated AI slop.
- Money ladder: YouTube ads ($1–3 kids RPM) → compilations/24-7 streams → DSPs → merch → streaming licensing. **License, don't sell.**
