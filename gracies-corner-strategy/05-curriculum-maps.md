# 05 — Curriculum Maps: Channel A (Sensory) & Channel B (Country)

*Week 1 deliverable. These maps drive the weekly Suno batches (every song must fill a slot), serve as the "educational value" evidence YouTube's kids-quality principles look for, and structure the catalog so compilations assemble themselves by theme. Names are placeholders pending the trademark knockout results.*

---

## Channel A — Sensory/Lo-fi (ages 0–18 months)

**Design rules (from the plan's honest-positioning stance):** genuinely slow pacing; high contrast but no strobing or rapid cuts; tempo <80 BPM for sleep content, 80–100 for awake content; long seamless loops; marketing copy speaks to parents about *calm and routine* — never developmental claims.

### Content pillars × audio moods

| Pillar | Visual content | Audio mood |
|---|---|---|
| **Dancing friends** (awake/play) | The character cast (fruits/shapes) bouncing gently to the beat | Lo-fi beats, 80–100 BPM |
| **Wind-down** (pre-nap/bedtime) | Slow drifting characters, dimming palettes, stars/moons | Gentle classical & lullaby arrangements, <80 BPM |
| **Focus/soothe** (fussy-baby reset, feeds) | Slow morphing shapes, high-contrast B&W→color transitions | Ambient pads, soft piano |
| **Routine markers** | Bath bubbles, morning sun, car-window rain | Matched to routine energy |

**Dancing friends (awake) — 14 slots:**
1. Apple bounce · 2. Banana wiggle · 3. Strawberry hop · 4. Orange roll · 5. Watermelon wobble · 6. Blueberry bop · 7. Circle song · 8. Square dance (gentle) · 9. Triangle tap · 10. Star twinkle-bounce · 11. Heart thump · 12. Rainbow parade · 13. All-friends dance party · 14. Peekaboo loop

**Wind-down / sleep — 12 slots:**
15. Goodnight friends · 16. Moon float · 17. Star field drift · 18. Sleepy ocean · 19. Rain on the window · 20. Snowfall hush · 21. Cloud ride · 22. Firefly glow · 23. Lullaby in the meadow · 24. Twinkle variations (PD melody, lo-fi arrangement) · 25. Brahms-style lullaby (PD, re-arranged) · 26. Hush little one (PD, re-arranged)

**Focus/soothe — 8 slots:**
27. Black & white beginnings (newborn high-contrast) · 28. Slow color bloom · 29. Gentle kaleidoscope · 30. Bubbles rising · 31. Feather fall · 32. Ripple pond · 33. Breathing shapes (in… out…) · 34. Soft spiral

### Format multipliers
Every slot yields: 3-min core → 10-min extended loop → slot into weekly 30-min themed compilation ("Sleepy Time Vol. 1") → monthly 1-hr and 2-hr sleep editions → 24/7 calm stream at ~3hr library. Sleep slots are also the Spotify play: "lullaby versions" as DSP releases targeting sleep playlists.

## Channel B — Country Toddler (ages 1–5)

**World:** an animated farm/ranch. Cast (working archetypes, final at character design): a kid protagonist + fiddle-playing dog + banjo bear or similar + a barnyard ensemble whose animal sounds are a *native* curriculum advantage.

**Design rules:** real country production (fiddle, banjo, acoustic guitar, brushed drums — never nursery-synth with a hat on); hook in first 10 seconds; one learning objective per song; 90–130 BPM.

### Learning pillars & slots (45)

**Classics rebuilt, country-style (PD melodies — launch accelerators, 8):**
1. Old MacDonald (the most natural fit in music history) · 2. Wheels on the Tractor · 3. She'll Be Comin' 'Round the Mountain · 4. If You're Happy Stomp Your Boots · 5. Row Your Boat (paddle down the creek) · 6. Bingo (the farm dog) · 7. This Little Light (porch-light version) · 8. Skip to My Lou (barn dance)

**ABCs & first words (6):**
9. Boots-on ABC (alphabet hoedown) · 10. A is for Apple-picking · 11. Animal-sound alphabet · 12. Howdy! (greetings song) · 13. Please & thank-you, partner · 14. Big words for little wranglers (opposites)

**Numbers & counting (5):**
15. Countin' chickens 1–5 · 16. Ten little fireflies · 17. Five speckled eggs · 18. Boots by the door (pairs/2s) · 19. Hay-bale stack (1–10)

**Colors & shapes (4):**
20. Red barn, blue sky · 21. Rainbow over the ranch · 22. Shapes in the barnyard · 23. Patchwork quilt song

**Farm animals & sounds (6):**
24. What does the rooster say? · 25. Cow says moo (call-and-response) · 26. Little duck parade · 27. Sheep gotta dance · 28. Horse trot rhythm song · 29. Barnyard band (every animal gets a solo)

**Routines (8):**
30. Rise & shine (morning) · 31. Brush-your-teeth two-step · 32. Bath after the mud puddle · 33. Veggies from the garden (eating) · 34. Boot scootin' clean-up · 35. Potty-time partner · 36. Sun's going down (wind-down) · 37. Goodnight, farm (lullaby — also the DSP sleep single)

**Feelings & social (6):**

**Movement & seasons (5):**
44. Boot Scootin' Baby (the flagship dance hook — lead single candidate) · 45. Clap, stomp, howdy! · 46. Harvest time · 47. Snow on the ranch · 48. Rodeo day (birthday/celebration song)

### Launch slate (first 10, weeks 6–9)
Boot Scootin' Baby (flagship) → Old MacDonald → What does the rooster say? → Boots-on ABC → Countin' chickens → Wheels on the Tractor → Rise & shine → Goodnight, farm → Barnyard band → If You're Happy Stomp Your Boots.
Rationale: two PD classics for search traffic ("Old MacDonald country"), one original dance hook for Shorts/TikTok virality, core curriculum coverage (ABC/counting/animals/routines), and a sleep song for the DSP side — every launch video seeds a different discovery channel.

### Spanish mirror priority (Phase 2)
Highest-transfer slots first: animal sounds, counting, colors, Goodnight farm — the visuals reuse 100%; only vocals regenerate.
